No mystery.

A considered approach to learning, experimentation, and building systems.

  1. Agree on the boundaries.

    Define the goal, the systems in scope, and what success looks like. Security testing starts with written authorization and agreed rules of engagement.

  2. Test or build with purpose.

    Focus on the paths that matter to the system. Keep access intentional, work reviewable, and surprises visible.

  3. Document what comes next.

    Record findings, explain the tradeoffs, and identify the fixes, validation, and follow-up that come next.
