No mystery.

The work should leave you with a clearer system and a concrete next step.

  1. Agree on the boundaries.

    Define the goal, the systems in scope, and what success looks like. Security testing starts with written authorization and agreed rules of engagement.

  2. Test or build with purpose.

    Focus on the paths that matter to your business. Keep access intentional, work reviewable, and surprises visible.

  3. Leave a practical handoff.

    Walk through the findings or integration, explain the tradeoffs, and identify the fixes, validation, and follow-up that come next.
